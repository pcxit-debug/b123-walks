# B123 Walks

Build a modern, simple, friendly and easy-to-navigate website for B123 Walks, a community walking group based in the West Midlands, UK.

ABOUT B123 WALKS

B123 Walks is a friendly community walking group bringing people together through walking, friendship, fresh air and exploring the countryside.

We organise regular group walks across the West Midlands and surrounding areas, including Worcestershire, Birmingham, Warwickshire, Shropshire, Staffordshire and the wider Midlands.

Our walks range from shorter, easier local walks to longer countryside adventures.

Our philosophy is simple:

Walk. Talk. Connect.

We want people to feel welcome whether they come alone, with a friend, are an experienced walker or are joining a walking group for the very first time.

A really important part of our ethos is:

No one left behind.

The website should feel like a welcoming community rather than a commercial walking company.

WEBSITE STYLE

Create a clean, modern outdoor design.

Use:

Large countryside photography

Walkers enjoying the British countryside

Green countryside and woodland imagery

Friendly, warm typography

Plenty of white space

Rounded buttons and cards

Clear navigation

Excellent mobile phone design

Use a colour palette inspired by the outdoors with deep navy blue, countryside green, white and subtle warm accent colours.

Avoid clutter, complicated menus, excessive animations or corporate-looking stock imagery.

The website needs to be extremely easy to navigate, particularly on mobile phones.

HOMEPAGE

Create a large hero section with a beautiful photograph of walkers in the Midlands countryside.

Headline:

Welcome to B123 Walks

Subheading:

Walking the Midlands. Making Friends. Creating Memories.

Supporting text:

Join our friendly walking community and discover fantastic walks across the Midlands. Come along, enjoy the countryside, meet new people and walk at a comfortable social pace.

Buttons:

View Upcoming Walks

Join B123 Walks

Below this, create a section:

Walking Together

Explain that B123 Walks is about much more than simply covering miles.

It's about getting outdoors, meeting people, improving wellbeing, discovering new places and enjoying a few laughs along the way.

Include three simple feature cards:

Great Walks
Regular organised walks throughout the Midlands.

Great People
A welcoming community where new walkers are encouraged to join us.

No One Left Behind
Our walks are social walks, not races. We walk together as a group.

UPCOMING WALKS

Each walk should have a card showing:

Walk title

Location

Date

Start time

Distance

Approximate duration

Difficulty

Walk leader

Meeting point

Short description

Button saying View Walk Details

Allow walks to be easily added, edited and removed later.

Include filters for:

All Walks | Shorter Walks | Longer Walks | Weekend Walks | Evening Walks

Make this extremely simple to use on a mobile phone.

ABOUT US

Create an About B123 Walks page.

Explain that we're a Midlands-based community of people who enjoy walking, friendship, countryside and getting outdoors.

People shouldn't worry about arriving alone. Many members originally joined without knowing anybody.

The atmosphere should be described as friendly, relaxed and welcoming.

Include our motto prominently:

NO ONE LEFT BEHIND

JOIN US

Create a simple Join B123 Walks page.

Explain:

New walkers are welcome to come along and see what B123 Walks is all about.

Membership is £15 per person per year.

New members can attend two introductory walks free of charge before deciding whether they would like to become members.

Explain that membership helps support the running of the group and its official organised walks.

Include a prominent:

Join B123 Walks

button.

Leave the button ready to connect to our preferred membership or payment system later.

WALK SAFETY

Create a simple Walk Information & Safety page.

Explain that walkers should:

Wear suitable footwear

Wear clothing appropriate for the weather

Carry sufficient water

Bring food/snacks when appropriate

Follow instructions from the walk leader

Tell the leader if they are struggling

Respect the countryside and other walkers

Explain that walk leaders may decide someone is not suitably equipped for a particular walk where there is a genuine safety concern.

Keep this friendly rather than intimidating.

Design it so photographs can easily be added later.

FAQ

Create a simple FAQ including:

Can I come on my own?
Absolutely. Lots of people join us on their own and quickly get to know people.

Do I need to be an experienced walker?
No. Check the distance and difficulty of the walk and choose something you're comfortable with.

Are your walks races?
Definitely not. We're a social walking group.

Where do you walk?
Across the Midlands and surrounding countryside.

How much is membership?
£15 per person per year, with two introductory walks available before joining.

What should I bring?
Suitable footwear, appropriate clothing, water and anything recommended for that particular walk.

IMPORTANT DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Prioritise mobile users because many members will access the website through links shared on WhatsApp and Facebook.

Make important information accessible within one or two taps.

Use large buttons and readable text.

Do not overload pages with information.

The most important actions throughout the website should always be:

Find a Walk

and

Join B123 Walks

The overall feeling should be:

Friendly. Local. Outdoors. Inclusive. Community-focused.

Visitors should immediately think:

"That looks like a friendly bunch of people. I'd feel comfortable going on one of their walks."
